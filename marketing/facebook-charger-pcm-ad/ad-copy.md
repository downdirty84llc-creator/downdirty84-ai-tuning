## What to test first

1. **Hook.** "2015 & NEWER / CHARGER & CHALLENGER" (platform-led) vs
   "YOUR PCM IS LOCKED" (problem-led) as the opening 2 seconds. Cheapest and
   highest-leverage test available.
2. **Price placement.** Leading the primary text with $650 vs leading with the
   unlocked-PCM problem. $650 is a considered purchase, not an impulse one, so
   leading with price may filter more than it attracts.
3. **Destination.** Messenger vs a landing page that collects the VIN up front.
   Messenger is lower friction; a form qualifies harder.
