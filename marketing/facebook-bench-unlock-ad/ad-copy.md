## Targeting to start

- North-east Georgia and Atlanta metro as one ad set: mobile service only
  works within driving distance, and that is the strongest version of this
  offer.
- A separate national ad set for mail-in, since the module travels and the
  customer does not.
- Interests: ECU tuning, PCM programming, automotive diagnostics, HP Tuners,
  locked PCM, module repair, independent repair shops.
- Worth testing against repair shops as well as owners — a shop that cannot
  unlock a module in-house is a repeat customer, not a one-off.

## What to test first

1. **Audience.** Owners vs independent shops. Shops are fewer but repeat.
2. **Hook.** "Off the car, on the bench" (what the service is) vs "Locked
   module?" (the problem). The second may qualify harder.
3. **Mail-in vs mobile.** Lead with whichever the ad set's geography can
   actually use, rather than showing both everywhere.

Change one at a time.

## Notes for whoever edits this next

The video is **silent**. There is no footage and no audio source for this
service, so it carries a silent track. Every claim is on screen as text, which
is what matters for muted autoplay, but it will feel flat with sound on. If
bench audio ever gets recorded, it is worth re-cutting.
